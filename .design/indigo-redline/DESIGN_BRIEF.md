# Indigo Redline — Design Brief

Barage Machinery is an operational system for machinery, locations, requests, users, service records, and inventory. The redesign must make dense daily work faster without changing Flask routes, permissions, database behavior, or Bulgarian content.

The approved direction combines graphite and indigo structure with Barage signal red (`#DC252E`) for primary actions, active navigation, focus, and important emphasis. Light and dark themes are equal products. Layouts use the full canvas, typography is compact and readable, important actions always have visible text, and decorative effects stay restrained.

Success means an operator can scan almost every important table field on a laptop without horizontal scrolling, understand status without color alone, and reach the next action without guessing an icon.

Responsive behavior: full compact tables from 1280px; selectively hide only low-priority fields from 900–1279px; use labeled cards below 760px. Keyboard focus, empty states, validation, and contrast must remain clear in both themes.
